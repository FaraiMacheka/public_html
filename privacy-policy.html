Website Policy

Harvest House International RSA Hubs (Jo'burg , Tshwane, Eastern & Western Cape ) operate multiple websites on behalf of the church. The Web Master is responsible for maintaining these pages .

  1. hhi.org.za
  2. School of Ministry
  3. Conference
  4. Worship altar
  5. Maximized lifestyle International

Policy

The Church encourages the appropriate use of the websites as a method for communicating church activities and information to the public. The primary focus is to communicate with church members, but the secondary focus is to recruit new members.

Content and information released on the website is equivalent in stature to content and information released to the media and public in any other format.

Photos or content of adult congregation members may be posted without consent, as the church is a public venue. However, if a congregation member wishes for their likeness to be removed from the website, the web master will do so at their earliest convenience.

Congregation members are encouraged to provide ideas for content to the web master, but the Social media Administrator is responsible for vetting and approving any ideas for content. Submitting content to the Social media Administrator does not guarantee it will be posted.

for content submission email the Social media Administrator

Social Media Policy

Harvest House International RSA Hubs (Jo'burg , Tshwane, Eastern & Western Cape ) operate multiple social media pages

  1. Facebook
  2. Instagram
  3. Twitter
  4. YouTube
  5. Pinterest
  6. Titok

on behalf of the church. The Social Media Administrator is responsible for maintaining these pages.

Policy

The Church encourages the appropriate use of social media as a method for communicating church activities and information to the public and for receiving responses and commentary from the public. The primary focus is to communicate with church members, but the secondary focus is to recruit new members through keeping an updated social media presence.

Members of our congregation are not authorized to represent themselves as an official spokesperson of Harvest House International RSA Hub on social media without prior approval from the Hubs (Jo'burg , Tshwane, Eastern & Western Cape ) leader .

Content and information released on social media is equivalent in stature to content and information released to the media and public in any other format.

Harvest House International RSA Hubs (Jo'burg , Tshwane Eastern & Western Cape ) reserves the right to remove posts or comments that are obscene, defamatory, offensive, threatening, abusive, spam, advertising a product or are unrelated to the content under discussion.

Photos or content of adult congregation members may be posted without consent, as the church is a public venue. However, if a congregation member wishes for their likeness to be removed from social media, the social media Administrator will do so at their earliest convenience.

Congregation members are encouraged to provide ideas for content to the social media Administrator, but the social media Administrator is responsible for vetting and approving any ideas for content. Submitting content to the social media Administrator does not guarantee it will be posted.

Congregation members are encouraged to "like"/"subscribe"/"follow" and engage with the Church's Social media pages, but are by no means required to do so

for content submission email the Social Media Administrator
