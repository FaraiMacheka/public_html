About The Apostolic & Prophetic Conference

The apostolic and prophetic conference is a special time for us to gather and receive ministry from our apostolic leaders, Bishop Dr Colin Nyathi and Bishop Dr Sarah Nyathi. This year is extra special as we expect a huge feast of the word of God, impartation and powerful ministry on the theme, Leadership and Humility. The launch of our second album, All about Jesus, will be the cherry on top. This is a special weekend and we can’t wait to enjoy it with you!
